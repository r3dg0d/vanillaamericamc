What the plus means

Vanilla at heart.
Community by design.

The landscape is yours to shape: ordinary gathering, building, redstone, exploration, and survival progression remain the center of play.

The “plus” is the civic layer around it—Java and Bedrock cross-play, a living map, transparent reports, reversible moderation, occasional cosmetic celebrations, and a shop that never sells competitive power.

Built for the long haul

A small, deliberate feature set.

01

True cross-play

Geyser and Floodgate bridge supported Bedrock clients to the Paper server without weakening Java account authentication.

02

Living atlas

BlueMap renders the world in 3D on a separate local route. If rendering is unavailable, the site remains usable.

03

Accountable staff

Reports and every staff outcome enter an immutable audit trail. Moderator and administrator authority remain separate.

04

Vanilla-first events

The Liberty Bell celebration uses restrained sounds, particles, and editable community notes—never destructive effects or paid power.

Community compact · version 1.0

Build freely. Treat people well.

  1. Respect the work.No destructive griefing, theft, or bypassing agreed boundaries.
  2. Respect the person.No harassment, hate, threats, doxxing, or sexual content.
  3. Play honestly.No cheats, exploit abuse, automation that harms service, or ban evasion.
  4. Keep it welcoming.Chat and builds must remain suitable for an all-ages community.
  5. Use the process.Report concerns with /report; staff actions are logged and reviewable.
